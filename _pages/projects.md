i have several projects that might or might not be math related.

## Mettamatika
anime-styled fanbook trilogy about mathematics by [spearhead circle](https://spearhead-circle.github.io/). vol. 1 is on calculus, vol. 2 on statistics, and vol. 3 on linear algebra. i also write a zine for mettamatika vol. 1.5 titled *Scara's Mathematical Adventure in Inazuma*, and can be accessed [here](https://spearhead-circle.github.io/blog/mettamatika-paruh/) (along with other titles).

## Genshin Impact Math Paper
i wrote a paper on a puzzled which appeared in one of the inazuma event from genshin impact, titled *General Solutions to the Light-Up Tile Puzzles*. it can be accessed [here](https://github.com/aris-uu/papers/blob/main/General%20Solutions%20to%20the%20Light-Up%20Tile%20Puzzles.pdf). a zine version is also available in the mettamatika vol. 1.5 above. i also presented this paper at Genshiken Staff Training Festival (GSTFest) 2023 [~~while cosplaying as bocchi from bocchi the rock~~](https://youtu.be/Eyf2hWbBM9c).

## Lean Laboratory Pilot Project
i once led a lean laboratory pilot project for students of the Foundations of Mathematics course in the short semester 2024/2025. students learn basic math proofing mechanics using the lean theorem prover. the lab module is available [here](/math-notes/Praktikum-Dasmat).
